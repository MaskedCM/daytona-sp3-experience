`daytona-sp3.glb` was extracted from the creator's original-format download without modification. `daytona-sp3-explodable.glb` regroups connected pieces into 19 presentation assemblies while retaining the original triangle geometry. `daytona-sp3-web.glb` additionally uses meshopt compression and geometry quantization for web delivery. These presentation assemblies are not verified engineering assemblies.
